---
title: "Bah, heisenbug!"
link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisenbug
---

A **heisenbug** is a software bug that seems to disappear or change behavior the moment you try to investigate it. Sometimes adding a log statement, stepping through a debugger, or making an unrelated code change is enough to make the problem vanish.

The name comes from Werner Heisenberg’s uncertainty principle: the principle in quantum mechanics that certain pairs of physical properties—i.e., a particle’s position and momentum—cannot both be known with arbitrary precision at the same time. In other words, the process of observing a system can sometimes influuence it.
