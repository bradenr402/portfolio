---
title: 'Focus and navigate the macOS Dock with the keyboard'
---

On macOS, you can focus the Dock without a mouse using either <kbd>🌐︎</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> or <kbd>⌃</kbd> + <kbd class="mr-px">F3</kbd>. Once the Dock is focused, you can navigate entirely with the keyboard:

- Use <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> to move between Dock items
- Press <kbd>⏎</kbd> to open an app, file, or folder
- Press <kbd>↑</kbd> on a folder to expand it
- Press <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> in a folder/stack to navigate items
- Press <kbd>⎋</kbd> to exit Dock focus
- On folders/stacks, use arrow keys to navigate items and <kbd>⏎</kbd> to open the selected item
- Use <kbd>Space</kbd> on files to Quick Look them
- Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>⏎</kbd> on a selected item to reveal it in Finder
